The first hour

Do these in order

Lineage Piece hands you nothing and expects you to route yourself. This sequence skips the two mistakes that cost new players the most time: over-farming a starter island, and spreading stat points across every channel.

1. Group, then codes

5 min

Like the game and join the Roblox group first — several codes check for it. Redeem through the Settings icon on the left of the screen: paste, press the enter arrow. Most codes have a minimum level, so re-run the list as you climb.

2. Pick one channel

Permanent-ish

Damage splits into Strength (M1s), Weapon (sword skills) and Power (fruit and spec skills). Every point, race, trait and outfit you pick later should feed the same one. Sword route → Weapon. Melee-style route → Strength.

3. Quests over grinding

+20% EXP

Island quests carry a 20% EXP bonus since the Kokushibo patch. Clear every quest on an island, then leave — mobs below your level pay almost nothing, and staying “safe” is the slowest route in the game.

4. Armament Haki early

No level req

A flat +25% damage for a Haki Book and 1,250 gems, with no level gate. The book is a 0.1% drop from any enemy, so it lands while you level rather than as a project.

5. Bank rerolls, don’t spend

Math

Light fruit is a 2% roll and the mythic race bucket is 0.25%. Rolling with 300 gems in the bank is how people end up on Bomb at level 900. Use the odds tool to size the stack before your first roll.

6. Chests, and their mimics

25%

Opening a chest has a 25% chance to spawn a Mimic; killing it doubles the drop chance. Rare is the first tier that always contains a reroll or Summon Core, so don’t crack Rare+ chests while you’re fighting something else.

Islands, in order

Route & purpose

Community guides disagree sharply on level bands — some list Dawnwind as 1–25, others as 0–250, and the game has re-tuned them across patches. Treat the band as a hint and the purpose column as the real instruction: leave an island once you have what it exists to give you.

Endgame zones (Legend’s Arena, Lineage Town, Fossil, Monument, Shinjuku) are hubs rather than steps — you rotate between them for keys, rerolls, mastery and skill-tree points rather than clearing them once.

Stats, ranks and currencies

Where power comes from

The four stats

Strength
Raw damage on Combat and Weapon M1s — the basic click attacks.
Weapon
Weapon skill damage. The sword-build channel.
Power
Combat, melee-style and spec skill damage. The fruit and fighting-style channel.
Health
Max HP. Enough to survive one boss rotation, no more.

Even splits feel flexible and farm badly. Pick the channel your best weapon or style sits on, then push it.

Rank buffs

Ranking your material tier is a permanent, stacking economy upgrade — roughly +10% EXP, coins, gems and luck per rank. Climb them deliberately rather than by accident.

CoalCopperIronGoldRedStoneLapis

Sources agree on Coal→Gold; RedStone and Lapis appear in later patch notes and their exact buffs are not consistently documented.

Currencies

Coins
Mob, quest and chest income. Weapon recipes eat millions of them.
Gems
Rerolls, recipes, artifact rolls. The real bottleneck.
Bytes
Simulated Sea currency, spent only in the skill tree.
Fragments
Sea milestones, including artifact sub-stat rerolls.

Chest economy

Boss farming rolls chests at roughly 7.5% Epic, 5% Legendary, 1% Mythical. A Mythical chest pays about 200K–350K coins and 900–1,100 gems — which makes boss rotations, not mob grinding, the gem farm.

Rare tier and above always contains a reroll item or Summon Core.

Devil fruits

4 fruits · 2 rerollers

Two NPCs in Lineage Town reroll your fruit — one takes coins, one takes gems — and you can roll as often as you can pay. Published rates sum to 97%, so either rounding or an undocumented pool covers the rest.

FruitTierRoll chanceWhy

Reroll price

Roughly 25,000 coins or 250 gems per roll. Coins are the cheaper currency to farm, gems the faster one to burn — and gems are also what recipes want, so prefer coin rolls when the queue is long.

Light is the outlier

Light is the only fruit with flight, and its damage and AoE match or beat the rest. That, more than raw numbers, is why every tier list puts it first — travel time between islands is a real cost.

Races

7 rarities · 19 races

Races are a passive-buff roll across seven rarities, from Common up to Exclusive. The six Mythicals share one 0.25% bucket, so you are not rolling for a specific mythic — you are rolling for any of them, then hoping it matches your channel. Exclusives aren’t rolled at all: they’re item hand-ins, which is why they’re the ones worth planning around.

RaceRarityBuffsBest for

Several styles gate their mastery behind a specific race — Aizen wants True Shinigami, Gilgamesh wants Demi God, Kokushibo wants Demon, Gojo [Shinjuku] wants Enlightened. Check the style you’re chasing before you spend a reroll you like.

Traits

Second roll layer

Traits stack on top of race with their own rarity ladder. You reroll them with Trait Reroll items — from Rare and better chests, plus a low mob and boss drop chance — at the Lineage Town NPC, not with raw currency.

TraitTierStanding

Reported trait magnitudes vary a lot between guides (one lists The Ruler of Death at +120% damage). Rankings are consistent; exact percentages are not — verify in-game before you build a rotation around one.

Weapons

Recipes & hand-ins

Endgame weapons are crafted at a named NPC, not dropped. Read the recipe before you commit: Solemn Lament consumes your Player’s Dagger, so the “cheap” legendary is actually a required intermediate step.

Fighting styles & mastery

11 styles

You spawn with Combat and buy the rest with boss materials. Mastery is a separate, much longer questline that unlocks the style’s final move — usually a damage-dealt milestone plus a kill tally, and often a race and title requirement on top.

Accessories

Outfit drops

Outfits carry Strength, HP, Weapon and Power bonuses, which is why the right one depends entirely on your channel rather than on a global ranking. Two are farmable at 20%; almost everything else is a 2% or worse boss drop.

AccessoryDrop chanceBest for

Guides split on the top weapon-build outfit — Solemn’s and Player’s both get named first. They’re close enough that the one you actually own wins.

Bosses & the keys that gate them

18 world bosses

Field bosses respawn on a 30–90 second timer and can be camped. The ones that matter are summoned: you either hand materials to a summoning NPC or open a sealed portal with a key, and the key is usually a low-percentage drop from an earlier boss. That chain is the real endgame progression.

BossWhereAccessWhy you’re here
Key drop rates swing wildly — the Cartenon Key is a 60% drop from Sung Jinwoo, while the Shrine Key is 1% from a Frostborn Snow Bandit. Plan the Shrine Key as a farming session, not a stop on the way.

Simulated Sea

Roguelike mode

How a run works

Rooms
13 per run, cleared in sequence
Cards
One pick per room, from a pool of 39
Sea levels
5, each a hard step up from the last
Death Match
Harder rooms, better rewards and stronger card odds

Cards are the point. A run is won by finding a combination that scales, not by clearing rooms carefully.

What it pays

  • Bytes — the only skill-tree currency.
  • Artifact rolls — spent with gems after a run.
  • Fragments — milestone track, including artifact sub-stat rerolls.

Because Bytes gate permanent stat nodes, Sea runs compound: every run makes the next island and the next boss cheaper.

Artifact sets

6 sets · 2pc / 4pc

Two pieces from one set give a small crit bonus; four pieces give the bonus that actually decides your build. Note how cleanly the 4-piece bonuses map onto the three damage channels — this is the system telling you to commit.

Set2-piece4-pieceChannel

Where they come from

Artifact trials, by difficulty: Easy pays only Rare, Medium is the first real Epic farm, Hard is mostly Epic with a small Legendary chance, and Extreme is the Legendary chase — where you still walk out with an Epic about half the time.

Reading a 4-piece

Titan Executioner’s +32.5% to bosses and Supreme Mastery’s +47.5% mastery skill damage are conditional, not general. They beat a flat 30% only if your rotation actually is boss-fighting or mastery-skill-heavy.

Skill tree

Monument Island · Bytes

A point-buy tree that runs on Bytes from Simulated Sea. Lower nodes are prerequisites, so you cannot skip ahead to the good ones — the order below is the cheapest path to a build that farms itself.

Tier I–III

Foundation

EXP, HP and damage lines. Buy damage first; EXP pays off only while you’re still levelling, and HP only matters up to surviving one boss rotation.

Tier IV

Branches

Splits into crit (chance and damage), HP, and economy — coins, gems and luck. Crit is the DPS branch; economy is what shortens every farm after it.

EXP branch

Early only

Worth taking before max level and close to worthless after. If you’re near the cap already, route those Bytes into crit and economy instead.

Accuracy & sources

Read this part

Lineage Piece has no official public database — the developer posts to a Trello and Discord, and everything else is community-maintained. This page cross-checks several of those and marks the places they disagree, rather than picking one and sounding certain.

Known disagreements

  • Max level: 6,000 in most guides, 7,000 in newer ones. Likely raised by a patch.
  • Island bands: two incompatible scales in circulation (1–25–75–150 vs 250–600–1000).
  • Observation Haki level: 1,500 in questline write-ups, 500+ in one overview.
  • Fruit rates: published figures total 97%.
  • Trait magnitudes: rankings agree, percentages don’t.
  • Weapon-build outfit: Solemn’s vs Player’s, depending on the guide.

Check these first

  • The in-game Trello and Discord, linked from the Roblox page — the only first-party sources.
  • Anything numeric here that a patch could touch: recipe costs, drop rates, level gates.
  • Codes, always.
