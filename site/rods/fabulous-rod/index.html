Fabulous Rod

RodsTier SStage 8 · Money meta

Awarded for finishing the final stage of the Fabulous Deity’s quest in the Calm Zone. You cannot even start it below level 1000.

Passive and notes

Tier lists put it in the top handful, with luck in the 250–300% band. For almost every player the honest note is that the level requirement, not the quest, is the project.
