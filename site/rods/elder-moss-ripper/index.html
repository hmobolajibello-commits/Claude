Elder Moss Ripper Rod

RodsTier SStage 8 · Money meta

The rod that defines endgame income. Its Lurking Mossjaw passive guarantees a high-value Mossy Great Gold Cursed Shark on roughly a two-minute cycle, which is why Castaway Cliffs is quoted at 15–25M C$ per hour with it and 2–4M without.

Passive and notes

Passive — Lurking Mossjaw: a guaranteed Mossy Great Gold Cursed Shark about every 2 minutes.

Understand what that means: the rate is a timer, not a luck roll. Your job at the cliffs is to never be idle when the timer comes up — which makes lure speed and short fights worth more than extra luck here.
