Fast Rod

RodsTier CStage 1 · Early

A lure-speed rod. Fish bite sooner, so you get more casts per hour — worth real money when you are farming common fish for C$, worth much less when you are hunting one rare species.

Passive and notes

Lure speed multiplies your throughput; luck multiplies your odds per cast. Early on throughput usually wins, because you are selling volume.

Optional. If money is tight, skip it and the Lucky Rod both, and save for the Steady Rod.
