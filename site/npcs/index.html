NPCs

15 pagesClick a column to sort
NPCWhereWhat they do
PierreMoosewood beachRuns the tutorial. Onboarding only — he is not a merchant and sells nothing.
MarcMoosewoodThe starter rod shop: Plastic, Carbon, Fast and Lucky rods. The Carbon Rod at 2,000 C$ is the only one worth your first purchase.
The MerchantMoosewood, village centreBuys fish. Every named island has one, so you are never far from selling.
The ShipwrightMoosewood docksSells boats, starting with the Rowboat at 400 C$. This is the NPC that opens the map.
The AppraiserMoosewoodRe-rolls a fish’s weight and mutation for a fee — a second chance at a better variant without catching a new fish.
AgaricMushgrove SwampAsks for one Alligator and hands over the Fungal Rod free. The best-value NPC in the game.
The BlacksmithRoslit BaySells the Steady Rod — the rod that ends the early game.
The Mine GuardStatue of Sovereignty, by the elevatorTakes a 400 C$ bribe and lets you down to the Keepers Altar. One-time, cheap, permanent.
CaleiaThe ArchSells the Destiny Rod for 190,000 C$ — but only once your total bestiary hits 70%.
Keeper of the SkySkycrestHolds the Crest Amulet and takes the ten Sky Crystals that open Abaia’s Chamber.
MerlinSunstone IslandSells Enchant Relics at 11,000 C$ and 10-minute Luck VI boosts at 5,000 C$. XP-farming write-ups also credit him with a +20% XP boost.
AlfieRoslit HamletThe Steady Rod sits beside him at 7,000 C$ — the rod that ends the early game.
The Travelling MerchantMoves aroundStock and location both rotate. The Midas Rod at 55,000 C$ is his headline item, which is why guides disagree about where the Midas Rod comes from.
Forge Master TorinSkycrestTakes seven Ancient Idol Charms and five Giant fish, and forges the Ancient Idol Rod.
Fruit Lover FrankSkycrestWants 25 tropical fruits, then a follow-up catch, then the Fruity Abaia — and pays out the Fruitline, the highest-luck rod in the game.
