Sea Overlord

Enchantsmythical

A flat 25% on weight, and weight is a direct multiplier on sale value — so this is a flat 25% raise on every fish you sell, forever, with no conditions attached.

Using it well

The least exciting exalted enchant and one of the best. It never misfires, never depends on a roll and never stops applying.
