Quantum

Enchantsmythical

The mutation-farming enchant. A quarter of everything you catch comes up mutated, which compounds with weight and attributes into sale values nothing else reaches.

Using it well

Put it on the rod you farm money with, not the rod you explore with. A 25% mutation rate is worth the most when you are catching high-base-value fish repeatedly — Calm Zone or Castaway Cliffs, not a bestiary sweep.
