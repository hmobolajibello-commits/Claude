The Ancient Archives

LocationsStage 6 · Endgame

The crafting room, and the part of Fisch that rewards planning instead of money. Ten rods are craftable here, from mid-tier utility up to genuine endgame — and the room does not exist for you until the Ancient Isle totem puzzle is solved.

What is craftable here

Resourceful · Seasons · Riptide · Voyager · The Lost Rod · Celestial · Rod of the Eternal King · Rod of the Forgotten Fang · Spiritbinder · Vineweaver — and the 15,000,000 C$ Requiem.
