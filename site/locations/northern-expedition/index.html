Northern Expedition & Northern Summit

LocationsStage 4 · Mid-late

The far-north block. Progression guides route through it for one reason: the Arctic Rod at Northern Summit is the standard buy between the free Fungal Rod and the Trident Rod, and it is the rod that makes the cold biomes comfortable.
