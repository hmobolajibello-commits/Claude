Terrapin Island

LocationsStage 1 · Early

An early island whose value is almost entirely bestiary. Guides that route players to the 70% Destiny Rod gate name the same four blocks every time — Moosewood, Roslit Bay, Terrapin and the Ocean — because they are large, easy and finishable with a rod you already own.
