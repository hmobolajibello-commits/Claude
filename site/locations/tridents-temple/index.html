Trident’s Temple

LocationsStage 4 · Mid-late

You come here to buy one thing. The Trident Rod is the most flexible mid-game rod in the game and its passive — a roughly 30% chance to apply the Atlantean mutation, tripling a catch’s value — means it partly pays for itself.
