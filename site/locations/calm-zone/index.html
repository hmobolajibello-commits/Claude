The Calm Zone

LocationsStage 8 · Money meta

The other half of the endgame money meta, and the one that does not depend on owning a specific rod. Its draw is the Crystallized Seadragon at roughly 26,100 C$ a catch on average — enough that a good session here funds an entire rod tier.

Cliffs or Calm Zone?

  • You own the Elder Moss Ripper → Castaway Cliffs, and it is not close.
  • You do not → the Calm Zone, which rewards the rod you already have rather than one specific drop.
