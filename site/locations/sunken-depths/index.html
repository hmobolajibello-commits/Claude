The Sunken Depths

LocationsStage 6 · Endgame

A deep zone with its own trial chain and its own rod shelf. It is also where a weak rod stops being a handicap and becomes a hard stop: down here the species outweigh anything from the early game.

Before you go

Check your max weight rather than your luck. Luck decides what bites; weight decides whether you land it, and no amount of the first fixes the second.
