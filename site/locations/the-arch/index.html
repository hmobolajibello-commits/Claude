The Arch

LocationsStage 6 · Endgame

The payoff for every easy bestiary entry you filled on the way here. At 70% total bestiary, Caleia will sell you the Destiny Rod for 190,000 C$ — cheap for what it is, and the clearest example in Fisch of collection work converting into gear.

The bestiary rewards, in order

  • 70% total — Caleia unlocks the Destiny Rod (190,000 C$).
  • 100% total — a cosmetic-tier reward; sources name the Aurora Bobber, or the Aurora Glow Lantern.

Chase 70% deliberately: finish whole easy locations rather than one hard fish everywhere.
