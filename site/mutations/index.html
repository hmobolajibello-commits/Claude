Multipliers as published by community mutation tables. Attributes (Shiny, Sparkling) are listed alongside mutations because they stack with them.
MutationMultiplierHow you get it
Aether12×The top standard mutation. Rare enough that most players see one a handful of times.
Tryhard10×Joint second among standard mutations.
Plagued10×Joint second, and the one people most often mistake for a debuff.
Atlantean3×The Trident Rod applies it at roughly a 30% rate — the only mutation you can reliably manufacture mid-game.
Midas2.5×Top of the common-tier multipliers, and the one the Midas Rod is named for.
Purified2.5×Shares the 2.5× band with Midas.
Sleet2.4×A cold-biome mutation — Snowcap and the northern water.
Electric2.1×Storm-adjacent. Weather changes what you can roll.
Aurelian2×Solid 2× band.
Lightning2×Storm weather. A Tempest Totem makes storms happen on demand.
Shiny1.85×An attribute, not a mutation: admin-event only, stacks with a real mutation, and — critically — cannot be lost when you appraise.
Sparkling1.85×The other admin-event attribute. Stacks with Shiny and with a mutation on the same fish.
Silver1.8×Common enough to see regularly on a farming session.
Studded1.8×Shares the 1.8× band with Silver.
Glossy1.6×Mid-band, unremarkable, adds up over a session.
Aurous1.5×The 1.5× band is where most of your mutated catches land.
Cement1.5×1.5× band.
Darkened1.5×1.5× band; night-adjacent.
Frozen1.5×1.5× band; cold water.
Mosaic1.5×1.5× band.
SubspaceunpublishedApplied by the exalted Quantum enchant at a 25% rate. The enchant is the reliable source; the multiplier itself is not consistently published.
Golden2×Doubles value. The Midas Rod applies it at roughly 60%.
