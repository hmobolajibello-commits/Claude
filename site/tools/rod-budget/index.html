Rod budget planner

Every rod in this wiki that carries a published price, sorted against what you have in the bank — what you can buy right now, and how long the next ones are away at your current earning rate.

Hourly rates are rough community figures, and rods without a published price are left out entirely rather than guessed at. Prices with a dotted underline on their own page are disputed between sources — budget the higher one.
