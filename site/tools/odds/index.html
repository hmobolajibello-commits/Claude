Drop & catch odds

Fisch does not publish most of its drop rates, so this tool does not pretend to know them. Pick one of the rates that is published — the rod and enchant passives — or type in the rate you believe, and it will tell you what a given number of attempts actually buys.

The gambler’s fallacy, stated plainly: every cast is independent. Forty dry attempts at 1% does not make the forty-first likelier. These numbers tell you how much patience a grind needs on average — not what you are owed.
