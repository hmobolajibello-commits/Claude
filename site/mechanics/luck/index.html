Luck — universal vs preferred

Mechanics

The most common gear mistake in Fisch is stacking the wrong luck. Universal luck raises your odds on everything; preferred luck raises your odds on something specific. A +300% preferred bait is close to worthless if you do not know what you are fishing for.

Choosing between them

  • Filling a bestiary block, or farming rarity for money → universal (Weird Algae, luck rods).
  • Hunting one named fish → preferred (Truffle Worm).
  • Farming volume for C$ → neither. Take lure speed and sell more fish per hour.
