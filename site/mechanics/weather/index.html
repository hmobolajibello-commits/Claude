Time & weather

Mechanics

Weather and time of day are not scenery in Fisch — they decide what is catchable. The important consequence is that a stalled bestiary is usually a weather problem, and weather is purchasable.

The totem shortcut

  • Fog → Smokescreen Totem
  • Rain and storms → Tempest Totem
  • Wind → Windset Totem
  • Time of day → Sundial Totem
  • Undo all of it → Clearcast Totem

Totems change the state for the whole server, which is why hunt organisation in Fisch is a totem logistics problem.
