The route, in one table

PhaseLevelThe one thing that matters
Phase 01–10Rowboat (400 C$), then the Carbon Rod (2,000 C$). Nothing else.
Phase 110–40Steady Rod, then Agaric’s free Fungal Rod. Open the Keepers Altar for 400 C$.
Phase 240–80Farm Forsaken Shores. Bank relics, enchant once, properly.
Phase 380–150Trident Rod, 150,000 C$. Its 30% Atlantean passive pays part of itself back.
Phase 4150–180Vertigo to 100%, The Depths Key, Rod of the Depths at 750,000 C$.
Phase 5180+Atlantis: five levers, the Kraken, the Kraken Rod.
Phase 6EndgameGreat Dreamer at 500,000 C$; an exalted enchant on the rod you keep.
Phase 7EndgameCastaway Cliffs or the Calm Zone, and the money stops being a constraint.
