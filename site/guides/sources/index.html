Accuracy & sources

Fisch has an official wiki and a large community of unofficial ones, and they disagree in exactly the places you would expect: prices, drop rates, and anything a patch has touched recently. This page says where.

How this wiki treats disagreement

Where sources conflict, both readings are shown and the figure is marked like this rather than smoothed into one confident number. Where sources are simply thin — the regular enchant pool, a good many shop prices, the acquisition routes for several late-game rods — this wiki says so instead of filling the gap.

Data was compiled in September 2026, immediately after the Skycrest update. Event content on this site (Skycrest, Fischfest 2026) has since closed on its published schedule; the systems those events introduced are treated as live.

On the rod list specifically: the community counts between 188 and 257 rods. This wiki documents 67 — every one it could find a real acquisition route or shop for. The rest exist; they are simply not documented anywhere this wiki could verify, and inventing plausible prices for a 750,000 C$ purchase would be worse than leaving them out.
