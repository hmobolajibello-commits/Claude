Codes

Codes in Fisch are short-lived — most are tied to an update or an event and stop working within weeks. These were reported active in early September 2026. Redeem them the day you read about them.

SkycrestIsInTheSky1,000 coins, Banana boat, 5× Icy Fisch’n Dots, 5× Coral Pearl, 5× Cotton Candy Pieces, 5× Tropical Fruit mix, random totem
Shady1,000 coins, Neongrinder skin, Chainsaw skin, 5× Golden Tentacles, 20× Shady Scrip, random totem
HumpbackAndMegamouth1,000 coins, 5× Golden Tentacles, random totem
Sovereign1,000 coins, Shiny Flopping Salmon companion, 5× Golden Tentacles

Redeeming

  1. Open Fisch on Roblox.
  2. Tap the Menu button.
  3. Go to Rewards.
  4. Type the code exactly — they are case-sensitive — and press Redeem.

Rewards land instantly. A code that fails has almost certainly expired rather than been typed wrong — one tracker counted 36 working out of 139 ever issued.
