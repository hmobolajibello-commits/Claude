12 beginner mistakes

Ranked roughly by how much time each one costs. Most of them are not skill problems — they are ordering problems.

#MistakeWhy it costs you
1Buying the Plastic or Training rodThey cost 900 C$ and 300 C$; the Carbon Rod costs 2,000 and outclasses both. Buying them means earning the 2,000 anyway, later.
2Not buying the boat first400 C$ opens the entire map. Nothing else you can buy at that price does anything comparable.
3Skipping AgaricThe Fungal Rod is free and beats the rods on sale at that stage. One Alligator in Mushgrove Swamp.
4Leaving the Keepers Altar unopenedA one-time 400 C$ bribe gates the whole enchanting system — and the King’s Rod is sitting down there next to a skeleton.
5Re-rolling an enchant you likedEvery roll overwrites the last one. Farm a stack of relics, roll until something good lands, then stop.
6Stacking preferred luck for general farmingA +300% preferred bait does nothing measurable if you are not hunting the species it prefers. Use universal luck for sweeps.
7Fishing wherever you happen to beBase species value is the biggest term in what a fish sells for. Where you fish matters more than what you fish with.
8Waiting for weatherFog, rain, wind and time of day are all purchasable as totems. Waiting is a choice.
9Appraising everythingAppraisal re-rolls mutation too, so it can make a fish worse. Appraise fish that already show a mutation.
10Ignoring the bestiary until it is asked for70% total unlocks the Destiny Rod and 100% of Vertigo is the only route to The Depths. Both are far more painful retroactively.
11Upgrading instead of masteringRod Mastery permanently buffs the rod you already carry. A mastered mid-tier rod can beat a fresh higher-tier one.
12Assuming a guide’s price is currentFisch patches often. Every figure on this site — including the ones without a warning mark — is worth a glance at the merchant before you commit.
