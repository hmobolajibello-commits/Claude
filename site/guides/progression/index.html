Progression roadmap

Eight phases from the Moosewood dock to the endgame money meta. Every step is one of the milestones the path planner reads, so ticking things here and answering the planner honestly should tell you the same story. The checklist saves in this browser.

0 / 19 done

Phase 0 — The first hour

Level 1–10
Everything here happens on or next to the Moosewood dock.
  • Buy the Rowboat — 400 C$
    400 C$ to the Shipwright. Until you own a boat, Fisch is one island; after it, island names reveal themselves as you sail near them and every location in this wiki becomes reachable.
  • Buy the Carbon Rod — 2,000 C$
    Fish the Moosewood dock with the Flimsy Rod until you have 2,000 C$ and buy the Carbon Rod. Do not buy the Plastic or Training rods on the way — that is 900 C$ you then have to earn again.
  • Clear the four easy bestiary blocks
    Fill the bestiary for Moosewood, Roslit Bay, Terrapin Island and the Ocean. These four are the largest, easiest blocks in the game and they are what carries you toward the 70% total that unlocks the Destiny Rod later. Doing them now costs nothing you were not already doing.

Phase 1 — Getting off the starter island

Level 10–40
One shop rod, one free rod, one permanent unlock.
  • Buy the Steady Rod — 7,000 C$
    The Steady Rod widens the shake window and adds enough resilience that you stop losing fish to the minigame. This is the rod that ends the early game.
  • Open the Keepers Altar — 400 C$
    Sail to the Statue of Sovereignty, go into the mines, and pay the NPC by the elevator 400 C$. That is the entire cost of permanently unlocking enchanting. There is no reason to still have this closed at level 200.
  • Take Agaric’s free Fungal Rod
    Sail to Mushgrove Swamp, talk to Agaric, catch one Alligator, and he hands over the Fungal Rod for free. Its luck passive beats anything you could buy at this point, and it costs nothing.

Phase 2 — Income and enchants

Level 40–80
Stop fishing wherever you happen to be and start farming on purpose.
  • Farm relics and enchant your rod
    Fish Enchant Relics in bulk — Ancient Isle if you can reach it, the Nurse Shark shallows in Roslit Bay if you cannot — then spend the whole stack at the altar in one sitting. Each roll overwrites the last, so stop the moment you land something worth keeping.
  • Set up a real money farm
    Forsaken Shores is the mid-game answer to both money and XP, which is unusual — normally those are different places. Fish the waterfall pond, not the open shore. This is where the 150,000 C$ for the Trident Rod comes from.

Phase 3 — The mid-game rod

Level 80–150
The Trident Rod is the point of this phase; the Destiny Rod is the bonus.
  • Buy the Trident Rod — 150,000 C$
    The Trident Rod is the correct target for any mid-game player who does not know what to save for. Its ~30% Atlantean passive multiplies catch value by 3, so it earns a good share of its own price back.
  • Reach 70% bestiary → Destiny Rod — 190,000 C$
    At 70% total bestiary, Caleia at The Arch sells the Destiny Rod for 190,000 C$ — cheap for its tier. Finish whole easy locations to get there; percentage is percentage.

Phase 4 — Vertigo and The Depths

Level 150–180
The hardest gate in the base game, and the one with no shortcut.
  • Complete the Vertigo bestiary to 100%
    A hard gate with no shortcut. Vertigo must be at 100% before The Depths Key can drop at all. Treat it as its own project rather than something that happens by accident.
  • Fish The Depths Key from the Strange Whirlpool
    With Vertigo at 100%, fish the Strange Whirlpool until you catch The Depths Key. It cannot be bought or traded around.
  • Buy the Rod of the Depths — 750,000 C$
    750,000 C$ for the Rod of the Depths. Bank the money before you go down — nothing in The Depths helps you earn it and the trip back out is long.

Phase 5 — Atlantis

Level 180+
The first content gated on your level rather than your wallet.
  • Reach level 180 for Atlantis — level 180
    Atlantis gates on character level as well as money — around 180. If your level is short, money will not substitute; go stack XP bonuses and level deliberately (see XP & levelling).
  • Atlantis: five levers, then the Kraken Rod — 1,333,333 C$
    Flick the five hidden levers — behind the Merchant, under the Cannon Hut, behind the Waterfall, on the Mountain Bridge, inside the Skull Cave — to open the Kraken Pool, and buy the Kraken Rod on the same trip. Budget the higher of the two published prices.

Phase 6 — Endgame gear

Endgame
Rods stop being upgrades and start being choices.
  • Buy the Great Dreamer Rod — 500,000 C$
    500,000 C$ at Cursed Isle for high lure speed, high luck and a duplication passive. Cheaper than the Rod of the Depths and arguably stronger — if you can reach Cursed Isle, this is the better buy.
  • Land an exalted enchant
    Exalted Relics roll from a small pool of much stronger enchants: Quantum (25% Subspace mutations), Sea Overlord (+25% weight, so +25% money forever) and Invincible (fish the hazard pools with any rod). Spend them on the rod you intend to keep.

Phase 7 — The money meta

Endgame
Where the account stops progressing and starts printing.
  • Finish the ladder: Wingripper or Chrysalis
    The Underground Music Venue holds the two rods that mainstream progression guides finish on. Owning one makes the other a sidegrade — put the effort into enchanting instead.
  • Move to the endgame money meta
    Two farms: Castaway Cliffs with the Elder Moss Ripper (15–25M C$/hr, because its Mossjaw passive is a two-minute timer rather than a luck roll), or the Calm Zone for the Crystallized Seadragon at ~26,100 C$ average with whatever rod you already own.
